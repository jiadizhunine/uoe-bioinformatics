# UoE Bioinformatics 2026/27

An unofficial reference for the University of Edinburgh MSc Bioinformatics: courses, assessments, past-paper links, exam-topic analysis, LLM difficulty ratings and course-selection considerations.

The complete guide is available in English and Simplified Chinese, in both Markdown and PDF. Markdown supports online reading and navigation; PDFs support offline reading, printing and sharing.

## Coverage

| Content | Scope |
|---|---|
| Courses and assessment | Credits, nominal study hours and assessment descriptions for 4 compulsory and 15 optional courses |
| Past papers | Original university archive and PDF links, distinguishing unavailable papers, main diets and resits |
| Exam analysis | Recurring topics, reused structures, changing exam conditions and evidence gaps |
| Course selection | Subjective LLM difficulty ratings, two credit combinations and risk considerations |
| Public student accounts | Public sources with dates and explicit limitations |

## Important Limitations

- **This is neither an official university guide nor a student survey.** Difficulty ratings on a 1–5 scale are LLM judgments, not grade predictions. Prior knowledge, interests and delivery arrangements matter.
- **MATH11205 Machine Learning in Python: this edition recommends against selecting it.**
- **Exams do not automatically make a course harder; coursework does not automatically make it easier.** Labs, group projects and long reports also require substantial time.
